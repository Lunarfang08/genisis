# Demo video script

1. **Premise (0:00-0:20):** Open the landing page. Explain that outcomes come from seeded rules, while AI is optional annotation.
2. **Zero-cost proof (0:20-0:50):** Launch Demo, note the disclosure, advance a month, reset, and reproduce the same values.
3. **Create (0:50-1:25):** Register, choose Resource Scarcity, adjust population/cities, and show the compute projection.
4. **Observe (1:25-2:15):** Open the premium dashboard, step time, inspect vital signs, history, AI activity, and the selectable city map.
5. **Analyze (2:15-2:45):** Show historical series and export JSON/CSV/summary evidence.
6. **Engineering (2:45-3:30):** Visit Architecture, Performance, and AI Transparency. Highlight PostgreSQL authority, outbox/SSE, worker leases, test gates, and honest scale limits.
7. **Close (3:30-3:45):** Show the release checklist and GitHub link. State the known limitations directly.
